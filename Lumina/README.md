# Happy Birthday Pyaari Didi ✨ | 1M+ Creator Queen Edition 🎂👑

An ultra-premium, interactive, and emotionally engaging birthday celebration web application created for **Annu Didi (Pyaari Didi)**, celebrating her special day and her **1,000,000+ YouTube Creator Milestone**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Storyline Journey

### 1. 💫 Cheeky Interactive Loading System
- Starts with *"Loading Birthday Surprise for Annu Didi... 🎁"*
- Cheeky pause: *"Wait... 'Annu Didi'? 🤔"*
- Heartwarming correction: *"No no... Sabse Pyaari Didi! 💖🌸👑"*
- Smooth progress bar with percentage counter and tap-to-enter prompt.

### 2. 📸 3-Photo Showcase with Custom Visual Effects
- **Photo 1 (Headset)**: 1M+ Golden Creator Milestone Card with live subscriber ticker, YouTube Play Button styling, and glowing neon badge.
- **Photo 2 (Candid Smile)**: 3D interactive tilt polaroid with customizable filters (*Golden Hour Glow*, *Dreamy Rose*, *Cyber Streamer*) and lightbox modal zoom.
- **Photo 3 (Flower in Hair)**: Dreamy glassmorphism memory card with flower aesthetics and sweet brotherly captions.

### 3. 🎆 Real-Time Particle Morphing & Fireworks Engine
- Cascading birthday wish stardust rain (`HAPPY BIRTHDAY`, `PYAARI DIDI`, `1M QUEEN`, `STAY BLESSED`).
- Mathematical typography morphing into:
  1. `HAPPY`
  2. `BIRTHDAY`
  3. `PYAARI DIDI`
  4. `1M QUEEN`
- Interactive click & drag fireworks with customized glyphs (`💖`, `👑`, `✨`, `🎂`, `🌸`).

### 4. 🎂 Interactive 3D Birthday Cake & Candle Blowing
- 3D CSS cake with berry toppings and golden ribbon.
- Click or tap the flickering candle to "Blow the Candle".
- Realistic flame extinction, smoke puff animation, screen dimming, and massive confetti/firework explosion with celebration fanfare.

### 5. 💌 Wax-Sealed Golden Envelope & Natural Heartfelt Letter
- Interactive wax seal that breaks open to reveal an unfolded parchment letter.
- Written in genuine, affectionate, and non-AI sounding Hindi/English lines honoring her 1M milestone, radiant smile, and elder sister warmth.

### 6. 🎵 Web Audio Procedural Synthesizer
- Synthesizes a warm, peaceful Happy Birthday melody + ambient lofi chords.
- Interactive harp chimes triggered on touch and mouse movement.

---

## ⌨️ Controls & Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Click / Drag on Screen**: Creates sparkling heart fireworks & plays harp notes.
- **`M`**: Toggle background music on/off.
- **`C`**: Launch Mega Fireworks & Confetti burst.
- **`R`**: Replay experience from the start.
- **`F`**: Toggle Fullscreen mode.
- **`ArrowRight` / `ArrowLeft`**: Navigate through Story Chapters.

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or run a lightweight local server:

```bash
# Using Python
python -m http.server 8080

# Using Node.js
npx serve .
```
Then open `http://localhost:8080` in your browser.
